Recipe of the week

Twenty-minute lemon pasta

20 min · Serves 4 · Vegetarian · One pan

The trick is starchy pasta water and cold butter, whisked off the heat until the sauce goes glossy. If your sauce looks broken, add a splash of water and keep whisking — it always comes back.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 lemons — zest of both, juice of one and a half
  • 60 g cold butter, cubed
  • 60 g parmesan, finely grated, plus more to serve
  • A big handful of black pepper
  • Salt, for the pasta water

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Save two cups of pasta water before draining.
  2. In the warm pan, whisk lemon zest, juice, cold butter, parmesan, and pepper with a ladle of pasta water over low heat.
  3. Add the drained pasta and toss hard, adding pasta water a splash at a time, until the sauce clings and shines.
  4. Taste for salt and lemon. Serve immediately with more parmesan — the sauce waits for no one.

Jill's note: Leftovers thicken in the fridge. Reheat with a spoonful of water and it tastes almost new.
