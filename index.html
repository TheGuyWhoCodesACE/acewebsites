Services

What we do

Business websites

Home, about, services, contact. Four pages. Enough for most businesses to appear in search and take a phone call.

Landing pages

One page, one purpose. Used for a product, a promotion, or an event. Loads fast, says one thing, has one button.

Maintenance

Text changes, image swaps, small additions. Billed hourly. Most clients need this once or twice a year.

Redesigns

Your current site, rebuilt. We keep the content that works and remove the parts nobody visits.

Domain & hosting setup

We register the domain, point the DNS, and host the site. You get the login details. Nothing is locked up.

Basic SEO

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, and a sitemap. The routine work. We do not promise first-page rankings.

Process

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Call

    Thirty minutes. We ask what the site is for and who is meant to read it. If we are not a fit, we say so.

  2. 2

    Quote

    A flat price and a delivery date, written down. The price does not move unless the scope moves.

  3. 3

    Build

    You get a preview link in week one. One round of revisions is included. Additional rounds are billed hourly.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We point the domain, check it on phones, and hand over the files and logins. Then we are done, until you need us again.

About

The firm

ACE Websites is a web firm. It builds websites. It was founded in 2026 and currently employs one person, who does the design, the code, and the invoicing.

The work is static HTML and CSS. This is a deliberate choice: static sites load quickly, cost little to host, and do not break when a dependency publishes a major version. If a project genuinely needs a database or a login, we will tell you and refer you elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?
One to three weeks depending on size, assuming content is supplied on time.
Do you write the copy?
We edit what you send and can draft pages for an additional $50 per page.
Who owns the site?
You do. On delivery you receive the source files and the account logins.
Do you offer monthly packages?
No. Work is billed per project or hourly. There is no retainer.
Do you work with clients outside the US?
Yes. All communication is over email and video call.
