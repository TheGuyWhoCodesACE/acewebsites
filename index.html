About

The firm

One person, doing the design, the code, and the invoicing. The work is static HTML and CSS — it loads fast, costs little to host, and does not break when a software dependency changes.

How long does a website take?

One to three weeks, assuming content is supplied on time.

Do you write the copy?

We edit what you send. Drafting pages from scratch is $50 per page.

Who owns the site?

You do. You get the source files and the account logins at delivery.

Do you offer monthly packages?

No. Work is billed per project or hourly. There is no retainer.
