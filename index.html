Questions

Straight answers

ACE Websites is a small practice — one person handling design, build, and billing, which is why quotes are fixed and communication is direct. The work is static HTML and CSS: fast to load, cheap to host, and easy for anyone to maintain later.

How long does a website take?

One to three weeks, depending on size, once content is supplied.

What if I don't like the design?

One revision round is included. Anything inside the agreed scope gets revised until it's right.

Who owns the website?

You do. The source files and the account logins are handed over at launch.

Do you write the content?

We edit what you send. Drafting pages from scratch is $50 per page.

Do you offer monthly plans?

No. Projects are flat-priced and updates are hourly. There are no retainers.
