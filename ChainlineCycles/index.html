How drop-off works

Drop-off

Three steps, no appointment

  1. 1

    Roll in before 17:00

    Weekdays, or Saturday until 14:00. Tell us what's wrong in your own words — weird noises welcome.

  2. 2

    We tag and text

    You get a text the same day with the job, the price from the board, and the pickup day. Repairs over $80 include a loaner bike.

  3. 3

    Ready text, ride home

    Test-ridden before you're called. Pay at pickup — card, tap, or cash.

The shop

Two benches, no showroom

Chainline opened in 2014 in half a former machine shop — enough room for two repair stands, a wheel-truing station, and a wall of tools. We don't sell bikes, so nobody here earns commission on a bike you don't need. Repairs, builds, and honest advice about what's worth fixing.

  • Both mechanics ABicycle mechanics institute certified
  • Wheel building by hand, spoke tension checked twice
  • Loaner bikes available for any repair over $80
  • Trade-ins welcome — we quote scrap value in writing
