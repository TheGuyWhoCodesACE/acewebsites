Practice areas

How we can help

Six areas of focus. If your matter falls outside them, we will tell you honestly and refer you to someone who fits.

Personal injury

Car and truck collisions, premises liability, and wrongful death claims. Contingency fee — no fee unless we collect.

Family law

Divorce, custody, support, and adoption. We litigate when we must and settle when it serves the family better.

Estate planning

Wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and probate administration. Plans written to hold up, not just to be signed.

Business law

Formation, contracts, partnerships, and commercial disputes for companies from two employees to two hundred.

Real estate

Closings, title issues, landlord-tenant disputes, and construction contracts for owners and developers.

Criminal defense

Felony and misdemeanor defense, expungements, and license hearings. Prompt, discreet, and trial-ready.
