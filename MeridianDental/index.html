First visit

Seventy-five minutes, start to finish

No mystery and no sales pitch. Here is exactly what happens, and how long each part takes.

  1. 15 min

    Check-in

    A short health history, your insurance card, and anything you want the dentist to know before she walks in.

  2. 25 min

    Exam and digital X-rays

    Dr. Reyes or Dr. Okafor charts every tooth and shows you the images as she goes. You see what she sees.

  3. 30 min

    Cleaning

    Scaling, polishing, and flossing with one of our three hygienists. Here only for a problem tooth? Skip it — just say so.

  4. 10 min

    Written plan

    What we found, what it costs, and what can wait. You leave with it on paper. Nothing gets booked you haven't seen a price for.

In pain when you call? Say so — we hold two emergency slots every weekday and almost always see same-day emergencies before 15:00.

About

Two dentists, one office, since 2012

Dr. Naomi Reyes opened Meridian on Alder Street after six years in a corporate chain — she wanted an office where the hygienist has time to actually clean your teeth and nobody is scored on upsells. Dr. Samuel Okafor joined in 2017, and the two have kept it deliberately small: two chairs, three hygienists, one front desk that knows your name.

We take most PPO plans and file the paperwork ourselves. If you don't have insurance, ask about the in-house plan at your first visit — it costs less than two cleanings on their own.

  • Drs. Reyes & Okafor, both DDS, both general practice
  • Three hygienists — you can request the same one each visit
  • Digital X-rays, about 80% less radiation than film
  • Nitrous available on request, no upcharge
