About

Family-run since 1999

Bill's Construction started as two people and a flatbed. Today it is eleven carpenters, three project managers, and the same rule we started with: do the job the way you would do it on your own house.

We pull our own permits, carry full liability and workers' comp coverage, and finish what we start. Most of our work comes from referrals, which is how we like it.
