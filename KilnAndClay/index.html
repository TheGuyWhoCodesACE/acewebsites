Your first term comes
with everything.

Clay, glazes, and firing are already in the class fee — bring an apron and short fingernails. The instructor demos at a wheel beside you for the first six weeks, then works the room while you throw. Pieces that survive the kiln are yours; the rest become reclaim and start again.
